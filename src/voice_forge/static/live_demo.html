What's actually happening

This page opens a WebSocket to ws://<same-host>/v1/tts/stream. The first frame is {"voice": "<id>"}; the server replies with a session JSON describing the audio format (24 kHz mono float32-LE PCM).

Then the page either sends your text all at once (trickle off) or chops it into N-char chunks with M ms gaps (trickle on). The server's SentenceBuffer drains complete sentences as they form, runs backend.synthesize() on each, and pushes the float32 PCM back as a binary WS frame. The browser appends each frame to a Web Audio scheduling queue so sentences play back-to-back without gaps.

Unlike the static ws-*/index.html audition pages — those play pre-recorded WAV files captured during a one-time audition run — this page does the whole stream live in your browser, every time you click Speak.
